# Basic First Aid (offline reference)

## Severe bleeding
1. Firm direct pressure with clean cloth; keep pressure, add layers, don't remove soaked ones.
2. Elevate limb above heart if possible. Spurting or soaking through = emergency.

## Hands-only CPR (adult, unresponsive, not breathing normally)
1. Call emergency services first (or send someone).
2. Push hard and fast, center of chest, 100-120/min, ~5 cm deep. Don't stop until help arrives.

## Choking (conscious)
1. Can't speak or cough forcefully -> 5 back blows between shoulder blades, 5 abdominal thrusts; repeat.
2. If unconscious: begin CPR, call emergency services.

## Burns
1. Cool running water 20 minutes. No ice, butter, or creams.
2. Remove jewelry before swelling; cover loosely. Face/airway/chemical/electrical/large = emergency.

## Anaphylaxis
1. Use the person's epinephrine auto-injector immediately if prescribed.
2. Call emergency services even if symptoms improve. Lay flat, legs raised; nothing by mouth.
